# External reading: How an AI-education evidence registry makes claims traceable

This is a short external reading attached to Module 13, after Lesson 20 on large language models and verification. AAB is not a lecture module and the reading is not an endorsement requirement.

## Read

1. [AABoard public evidence registry and workflow](https://www.aaboard.org/)
2. [AAB research briefs and dataset documentation](https://www.aaboard.org/research)
3. Choose one public case record and inspect its structured fields. A useful starting example is [AI Audit: A Card Game to Reflect on Everyday AI Systems](https://www.aaboard.org/cases/AAB-CASE-2026-RV-116).

## Reading purpose

The course has emphasized that generated prose is not the same as verified evidence. This reading shows one public attempt to make an evidence claim inspectable by separating:

- the source publication from a registry summary;
- author-reported findings from reviewer interpretation;
- observed outcomes from causal claims;
- evidence type and confidence from publicity language;
- transfer conditions, limitations, and missing information from recommendations;
- a candidate contribution from an editorially reviewed public record.

## Reading check

Bring a short trace with four entries:

| Entry | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Registry claim | Copy or paraphrase one material claim from the chosen record. |
| Primary-source location | Give the paper section, page, table, or figure that supports or challenges it. |
| Status | Classify it as author report, reviewer inference, missing/unclear, or unsupported by the source/design. |
| Revision | State how you would rewrite the claim so its certainty matches the evidence. |
